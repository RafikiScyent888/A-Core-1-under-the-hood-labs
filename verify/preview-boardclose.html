Main logic board, close up — 63 primitives across four materials

Five shield cans each with the fence of clips the lid snaps onto; five connectors each with the latch bar a technician lifts; the SIM reader and its contacts; two gold antenna feed pads; six mounting screws with driver slots; passives in the clusters they actually sit in. Four parts, because it is four materials: green laminate on the pcb painter (glass weave, ground pour, 45° traces), bare steel cans, black connector bodies, gold pads.
