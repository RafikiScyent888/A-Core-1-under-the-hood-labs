Choose the panel for the job — four panels, turned to show what actually differs

Straight on they are identical, which is the point of them. TN is turned 52° and has washed out, VA 30° and is holding, IPS and OLED 10° and have barely changed — and the OLED's face is near-black because its black is the pixel switched off. Response time and refresh rate are deliberately not drawn: a still picture cannot show motion.

The meeting room projector — lens, filter, lamp door, exhaust

Intake one side, exhaust the other. Two of these four are consumables and one of them is why a projector shuts down twenty minutes into a meeting.

The back of a UPS — and the row that does nothing in a cut

Two banks of sockets, inches apart, identical to look at, and every plug fits both. One holds up when the mains fails; one is only protected against spikes.
